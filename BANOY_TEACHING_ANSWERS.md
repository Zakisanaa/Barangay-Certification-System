## 21. Request status meanings

**Topic title:** Request status meanings

**Resident question:** What do the request statuses mean?

**Approved answer:**

```text
Pending: waiting for staff review. Approved: staff approved the request. Completed: staff marked it complete under fulfillment rules. Rejected: staff declined it with a reason. Cancelled: an authorized cancellation was recorded. No-show: staff recorded a missed approved appointment after its slot ended. Expired: an eligible unfulfilled Pending request passed its scheduled day. Open the request for history and reasons.
```
